# EHS Risk Analysis Prompt

Analyze the user's EHS scenario using the following method:

## Step 1: Identify the Scenario

Classify the scenario, such as:

- Confined Space Work
- Hot Work
- Working at Height
- Temporary Electrical Work
- PPE
- Hazard Rectification
- Chemical Handling
- New Employee EHS Training

## Step 2: Identify Hazards

List possible unsafe conditions, unsafe behaviors, and management gaps.

## Step 3: Estimate Consequences

Explain what could happen if the hazard is not controlled, such as injury, fire, explosion, poisoning, electric shock, environmental release, or blocked emergency response.

## Step 4: Recommend Controls

Prioritize practical controls:

1. Stop or isolate unsafe work when needed.
2. Remove or reduce the hazard.
3. Add engineering or physical controls.
4. Add management controls, supervision, permits, and training.
5. Use appropriate PPE.
6. Confirm emergency response readiness.

## Step 5: Close the Loop

Recommend responsible owner, completion deadline, re-inspection, and long-term prevention when appropriate.

Do not fabricate legal standards, company rules, or inspection results.
